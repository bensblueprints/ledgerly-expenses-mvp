# Ad Copy — Ledgerly

## Facebook / Instagram ads

### Ad 1 — the math hook
**Hook:** Expensify costs $180 over 3 years (solo user). Ledgerly costs $29. Once.
**Body:** Ledgerly is a self-hosted expense tracker with local receipt OCR — categories, budgets with alerts, multi-currency, recurring expenses, CSV/PDF reports. Your receipts never leave your machine. No monthly fee, no per-seat pricing.
**CTA:** Pay once → own it forever

### Ad 2 — the freelancer angle
**Hook:** Your receipts are your business's paper trail. Why is a SaaS company storing them?
**Body:** Freelancers & small businesses: track expenses by project and client, snap a receipt photo and let local OCR fill in the amount/vendor/date, export a clean CSV or PDF for your accountant at tax time. Runs on your laptop or a $5 VPS.
**CTA:** Own your books — $29 one-time

### Ad 3 — the "cloud upload" angle
**Hook:** Every expense app wants your receipt photos in their cloud. This one doesn't.
**Body:** Ledgerly runs tesseract.js OCR right in your browser — no receipt image is ever uploaded anywhere. One SQLite file holds everything: expenses, budgets, categories, currencies. Back it up to a USB stick. Open source (MIT), one-time price.
**CTA:** Get the 1-click installer
